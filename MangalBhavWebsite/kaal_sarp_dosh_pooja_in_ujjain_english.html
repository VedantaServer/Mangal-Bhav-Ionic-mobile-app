Understanding the Dosh

🐍 What is Kaal Sarp Dosh?

According to Vedic astrology, when all the planets in a birth chart are positioned between Rahu and Ketu, this planetary combination is called Kaal Sarp Dosh.

Many Acharyas believe this combination can manifest in life as various obstacles, delays, or mental unrest, as a result of past karma. In such cases, worship of Lord Shiva, the Naga deities, and the Navagrahas — performed with faith, resolve, and scriptural procedure — holds special significance.

Kaal Sarp Dosh is classified into twelve major types. Its effect can vary for each individual according to their birth chart, so an examination of the kundli before the pooja is considered essential. Tap any house on the wheel to learn about each type.
