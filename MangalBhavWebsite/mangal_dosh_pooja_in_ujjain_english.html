Understanding Mangal Dosh

🔴 What Exactly Is Mangal Dosh?

According to Vedic astrology, when Mars (Mangal) sits in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house of a person's birth chart, it is referred to as Mangal Dosh or Manglik Dosh.

Its effect is most commonly seen in married life — recurring delays in marriage, friction with a life partner, or an unusually short temper and impatience. But not every Manglik chart carries the same intensity — the real impact depends on which sign Mars occupies and which planets aspect it.

Mangalnath Mandir in Ujjain is regarded as the most sacred place to pacify this dosh, since tradition says Mars itself was born here, on the banks of the Shipra. Click on any house below to see its effect.
