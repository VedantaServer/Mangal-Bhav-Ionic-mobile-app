Understanding Navagraha Dosh

🔴 What exactly is Navagraha Dosh?

In Vedic astrology, the positions of Surya, Chandra, Mangal, Budh, Guru, Shukra, Shani, Rahu and Ketu — these nine planets — shape the direction of our lives. When a planet is weak, retrograde, debilitated or placed in an enemy sign in the birth chart, its effect begins to show up in work, health, relationships and peace of mind.

This effect differs for every individual — which planet is afflicted and in which house it sits determines the kind of obstacles that show up in life. Click on any planet below to learn what its weakness could indicate.
