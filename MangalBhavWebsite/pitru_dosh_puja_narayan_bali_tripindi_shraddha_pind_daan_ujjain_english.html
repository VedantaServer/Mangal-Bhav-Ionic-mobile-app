Understanding Pitru Dosh

🔴 What exactly is Pitru Dosh?

In Vedic astrology, Pitru Dosh is said to arise when the Sun or the 9th house (the house of father and ancestors) in a birth chart is afflicted by Rahu, Ketu or Saturn, or when the lineage carries the weight of rites that were never completed for a departed soul.

Its effects are most often felt in delayed marriage, recurring health concerns without a clear cause, financial instability despite hard work, or unexplained tension within the family. Frequently Asked Questions

01Does Pitru Dosh mean my family is cursed?+

No. Pitru Dosh simply points to an ancestor whose rites were left incomplete, not a permanent curse. Performing Narayan Bali, Tripindi Shraddha or Pind Daan as prescribed is believed to bring the resolution these rituals are meant for.

02How do I know if my chart has Pitru Dosh?+

Share your date, time and place of birth at the time of booking on mangalbhav.com — our astrologer will study your chart and tell you the exact position.

03What is the difference between Narayan Bali and Tripindi Shraddha?+

Narayan Bali is performed for a soul that died an untimely or unnatural death and needs a specific rite for moksha. Tripindi Shraddha addresses three broader categories of unliberated ancestors together, through three pindas. Many families perform both in the same sitting for complete peace.

04How long does the puja take?+
